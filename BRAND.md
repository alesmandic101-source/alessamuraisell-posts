# @alessamuraisell — brand and posting rules

Read this before making any post. Everything in this repo is public.

## Who
Aleš Mandič, Slovenia. Builds digital products (guides, templates, mini-courses) for Instagram creators from the questions their audience keeps asking. The creator stays the face; Aleš builds. Plays pool for the Slovenian national team (European Championship, teams).

## Audience
Creators with 5k–100k followers who check this profile after Aleš DMs them. Posts must make him look trustworthy, warm and competent. They are NOT aimed at people who want to learn "shadow operating" — never use that term, never sell a course, never talk about "making money online".

## Voice (captions and slide text)
- English. Short sentences. Warm, professional, a little playful. Sounds like a real person, not a marketer.
- 1–3 emojis per caption, never more (he uses 🎱 🔥 👋 😂 📌).
- No hype, no invented numbers, results, clients or testimonials. No "game-changer", "unlock", "level up", "in today's world".
- End with one soft call to action: a question for comments, "save this", or "DM me".
- Caption length: 60–150 words. 0–3 hashtags max, optional.

## Look (keep it identical across posts)
- Colors: cream #F2ECE2 (background), ink #1C2321 (text), felt green #24493F (serious slides), seal red #B23A22 (the "A" seal + accents), light pink #F4C3B6 (accent on green), muted green text #C9D6CF on green.
- Fonts: Fraunces (serif) for headlines, often with one italic phrase; Inter for small text and labels.
- Every slide: red "A" seal top-left, @alessamuraisell bottom-left, slide count "1 / 3" bottom-right on carousels.
- His face on every post: big photo, small round badge, or full-bleed — vary it.
- Format: 1080×1350 (4:5) JPEG. Carousels of 3 slides (2–5 allowed).
- Vary layouts between consecutive posts (photo-led, text-led, list, quote). Same family, never copies.
- Start from templates/*.html, copy into posts/<NN-short-name>/slide-N.html, change only the text (and photo if needed). Photos are referenced as ASSET/<file>.

1. Value tip for creators (how to spot product demand in comments, what to sell, pricing basics, launch mistakes)

3. Personal / human (pool, family life without showing kids' faces, travel, food) tied back to a lesson
